Politika privatnosti — eZgrada

Verzija 2.0 · Datum poslednje izmene: 18.05.2026.

1. Ko smo

Aplikacija eZgrada (u daljem tekstu: "Aplikacija") je softver za upravljanje stambenom zajednicom koji omogućava stanarima, članovima saveta i upravnicima da komuniciraju, prijavljuju kvarove, glasaju, prate finansije zajednice i pristupaju važnim kontaktima i dokumentima.

Pružalac usluge (Rukovalac platforme):
Nikola Pavlović
Email: n.pavlovic785@gmail.com

Podela odgovornosti: Operater Aplikacije je rukovalac za podatke vezane za funkcionisanje platforme. Svaka stambena zajednica je samostalan rukovalac za podatke svojih stanara — eZgrada je u tom smislu obrađivač koji pruža tehničku infrastrukturu.

2. Koje podatke prikupljamo

2.1. Podaci o korisniku

Pri registraciji:

  • Ime i prezime
  • Email adresa
  • Lozinka (čuvana isključivo u kriptografski hashovanom obliku, nikad u izvornom obliku)
  • Telefon (opciono)
  • Uloga u zajednici (stanar, član saveta, upravnik, super-administrator)
  • Status verifikacije (na čekanju, odobren, odbijen)

2.2. Podaci o vlasništvu i stanu

  • Broj stana, sprat, kvadratura
  • Mesečna naknada za održavanje (ako je postavljena)
  • Tip stanara (vlasnik, zakupac)

2.3. Sadržaj koji generišeš

  • Prijave kvarova (opis, lokacija, prioritet, kategorija)
  • Fotografije priložene uz prijave (opciono)
  • Komentari na prijave
  • Glasovi na anketama (anonimizovani u prikazu rezultata)
  • Kontakti majstora koje predlažeš

2.4. Finansijski podaci (novo, V1.1)

  • Tvoje uplate prema zajednici (iznos, datum, poziv na broj, opis)
  • Tvoja zaduženja (mesečna naknada, posebni doprinosi)
  • Saldo i istorija plaćanja vezana za tvoj stan
  • Bankovni izvodi (CSV) — upravnik uvozi CSV izvod od banke; iz njega se preuzimaju samo uplate koje pripadaju tvojoj zajednici. Originalne CSV datoteke se NE čuvaju na serveru; samo se parsiraju i kreiraju pojedinačne transakcije
Ko vidi tvoje finansije: Upravnik i član saveta vide sve transakcije zajednice (član saveta samo gleda, ne menja). Drugi stanari NE vide tvoje pojedinačne uplate — vide samo ukupni saldo zajednice i svoje lične transakcije.

2.5. Dokumenti zajednice

  • PDF dokumenti koje upravnik uploaduje (ugovori, zapisnici sa skupština, finansijski izveštaji, pravilnici)
  • Vidljivi svim stanarima iste zajednice

2.6. Komšije i kontakti

  • U sekciji "Komšije" stanari iste zajednice vide ime, prezime i ulogu drugih stanara
  • Telefon se NE deli ako si običan stanar
  • Telefon se DELI sa stanarima zajednice samo ako si član saveta ili upravnik (javna funkcija)

2.7. Tehnički podaci

  • IP adresa pri prijavi (radi bezbednosne provere)
  • Tip uređaja i verzija operativnog sistema
  • Vreme prijave i odjave
  • Token za push notifikacije (vezan za tvoj uređaj, opciono)

2.8. Šta NE prikupljamo

  • Tvoju tačnu geografsku lokaciju (GPS)
  • Kontakte sa tvog telefona
  • Aktivnost u drugim aplikacijama
  • Identifikator reklama (IDFA)
  • Podatke o plaćanju karticom (Aplikacija ne procesuira kartična plaćanja)

4. Sa kim delimo podatke

4.1. U okviru tvoje zajednice

  • Tvoje ime, prezime i broj stana vidljivi su drugim odobrenim članovima tvoje zajednice
  • Tvoj telefon deli se sa drugim stanarima samo ako si član saveta ili upravnik
  • Tvoje prijave i komentari vidljivi su svim članovima tvoje zajednice
  • Tvoji glasovi su anonimizovani — drugi članovi vide ukupan broj glasova po opciji, ne ko je glasao za šta. Upravnik vidi pojedinačne glasove samo radi prebrojavanja
  • Tvoje pojedinačne finansijske transakcije vide samo upravnik i član saveta — ne drugi stanari

4.2. Sa drugim zajednicama

Tvoji podaci NIKADA se ne dele sa drugim stambenim zajednicama. Aplikacija koristi tehnologiju izolacije podataka na nivou baze (Row Level Security) koja garantuje da podaci jedne zajednice ne mogu fizički da budu pristupljeni od strane korisnika druge zajednice.

4.3. Sa trećim stranama

Podatke ne prodajemo, ne iznajmljujemo, niti prosleđujemo trećim stranama u marketinške svrhe. Ne koristimo eksterne analitičke alate (Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, sl.). Podatke prosleđujemo samo:

  • Supabase Inc. (naš hosting servis i obrađivač) — podaci se čuvaju u Frankfurtu (EU). Supabase je certifikovan za GDPR.
  • Apple Inc. — token za push notifikacije (kad uključimo notifikacije) prolazi kroz Apple Push Notification Service
  • Nadležnim državnim organima — samo na osnovu zakonske obaveze (sudski nalog ili zahtev nadležnog organa)

5. Gde i kako čuvamo podatke

  • Serveri: Supabase Inc., EU-Frankfurt (Nemačka)
  • Transport: TLS 1.3 enkripcija
  • Lozinke: bcrypt hash (nije moguće ih dešifrovati)
  • Pristup bazi: Row Level Security politike + JWT autentifikacija
  • PDF dokumenti i fajlovi: izolovan storage bucket po zajednici, autentifikovan pristup
  • Backup: Supabase point-in-time recovery (7 dana)

Privacy Policy — eZgrada (English)

Version 2.0 · Last updated: 18 May 2026

1. Who we are

The eZgrada application (hereinafter: "the App") is a software platform for housing community management, enabling residents, council members and building managers to communicate, report issues, vote, track community finances, and access important contacts and documents.

Service Provider (Platform Controller):
Nikola Pavlović
Email: n.pavlovic785@gmail.com

Responsibility split: The App operator is the controller for platform-related data. Each housing community is an independent controller for its residents' data — eZgrada acts as a processor providing the technical infrastructure.

2. What data we collect

2.1. User data

At registration:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored exclusively in cryptographically hashed form, never in plain text)
  • Phone (optional)
  • Role within the community (resident, council member, manager, super-admin)
  • Verification status (pending, approved, rejected)

2.2. Property and apartment data

  • Apartment number, floor, square meters
  • Monthly maintenance fee (if set)
  • Resident type (owner, tenant)

2.3. User-generated content

  • Issue reports (description, location, priority, category)
  • Photos attached to reports (optional)
  • Comments on reports
  • Votes in polls (anonymized in results)
  • Contractor recommendations you submit

2.4. Financial data (new, V1.1)

  • Your payments to the community (amount, date, reference number, description)
  • Your charges (monthly fee, special contributions)
  • Balance and payment history linked to your apartment
  • Bank statements (CSV) — the manager imports a CSV statement from the bank; only payments belonging to your community are extracted. Original CSV files are NOT stored on the server; only parsed and individual transactions are created
Who can see your finances: The manager and council members see all community transactions (council members view-only, cannot modify). Other residents CANNOT see your individual payments — they only see the community's total balance and their own personal transactions.

2.5. Community documents

  • PDF documents uploaded by the manager (contracts, meeting minutes, financial reports, rulebooks)
  • Visible to all residents of the same community

2.6. Neighbors and contacts

  • In the "Neighbors" section, residents of the same community can see other residents' name, surname and role
  • Phone is NOT shared if you are an ordinary resident
  • Phone IS shared with community residents only if you are a council member or manager (publicly elected role)

2.7. Technical data

  • IP address at login (for security verification)
  • Device type and operating system version
  • Login and logout timestamps
  • Push notification token (linked to your device, optional)

2.8. What we do NOT collect

  • Your precise geographic location (GPS)
  • Contacts from your phone
  • Activity in other applications
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • Card payment data (the App does not process card payments)

Legal framework: Personal Data Protection Act of the Republic of Serbia ("Official Gazette of RS", No. 87/2018) and EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Art. 6.

4. Whom we share data with

4.1. Within your community

  • Your name, surname and apartment number are visible to other approved members of your community
  • Your phone is shared with other residents only if you are a council member or manager
  • Your reports and comments are visible to all members of your community
  • Your votes are anonymized — other members see total counts per option, not who voted for what. The manager sees individual votes only for tallying purposes
  • Your individual financial transactions are visible only to the manager and council members — not to other residents

4.2. With other communities

Your data is NEVER shared with other housing communities. The App uses database-level data isolation technology (Row Level Security) that guarantees data of one community cannot be physically accessed by users of another community.

4.3. With third parties

We do not sell, rent, or forward data to third parties for marketing purposes. We do not use external analytics tools (Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, etc.). Data is forwarded only to:

  • Supabase Inc. (our hosting and processing service) — data is stored in Frankfurt (EU). Supabase is GDPR-certified.
  • Apple Inc. — push notification token (when we enable notifications) passes through the Apple Push Notification Service
  • Competent state authorities — only based on legal obligation (court order or request from competent authority)

5. Where and how we store data

  • Servers: Supabase Inc., EU-Frankfurt (Germany)
  • Transport: TLS 1.3 encryption
  • Passwords: bcrypt hash (cannot be decrypted)
  • Database access: Row Level Security policies + JWT authentication
  • PDF documents and files: isolated storage bucket per community, authenticated access
  • Backup: Supabase point-in-time recovery (7 days)

6. How long we retain data

After that period, those records are also deleted.

7. Your rights (GDPR + Serbian PDPA)

  1. Right of access — you can see all your data through the App (Profile, Account, My apartments). Additionally, we send a copy of all data on request.
  2. Right to rectification — directly in the App (Profile) or via manager contact
  3. Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — directly in the App (Profile → Delete Account), with the exception of the legal obligation for financial records
  4. Right to portability — copy in machine-readable format (JSON)
  5. Right to object — you can object to processing
  6. Right to withdraw consent — by deleting your account or through Settings → Notifications for push

To exercise any right: n.pavlovic785@gmail.com

We will respond within 30 days.

8. Account deletion

You can permanently delete your account in the App via: Profile → Delete Account.

Account deletion:

  • Permanently erases all your personal data (name, email, phone, profile)
  • Erases all your reports, comments, votes
  • Erases all photos you submitted
  • Erases all associations with apartments you are registered in
  • Financial transactions are anonymized (instead of deleted) due to the Accounting Act — after 10 years they are also deleted

Personal data deletion is irreversible.

9. Data security

We take industry-standard protection measures:

  • TLS 1.3 for all traffic
  • bcrypt password hashing
  • Row Level Security at the database level (each community has isolated access)
  • JWT tokens with automatic expiration
  • iOS Keychain for storing session tokens on the device
  • Hardware-backed data encryption on the iPhone device
  • Authenticated access to PDF documents (Storage RLS)

10. Minors

The App is not intended for persons under 16 years of age. A housing community is a legal form for adult owners and residents. If we learn that we have data of a child under 16, we delete it within 7 days.
